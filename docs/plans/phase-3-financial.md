## 0. Rules that bind every task

Phase 1 §0 rules 1–10 and Phase 2 §0 rules 11–17 apply unchanged, with the wave process from
`WORKLOG.md` ("Process since 2026-10-03"): groundwork commit, waves, one review round per wave,
one full `phase-gate` at slice 28. Additions for Phase 3:

18. **Money is integers.** Every amount column is `integer` whole rupees (rule 15) with a named
    CHECK; no `numeric`, no `float`, no paisa, no `Decimal` in TypeScript; one shared
    `MAX_RUPEES` bounds every DTO. A percentage concession is applied once, rounded **down** to a
    whole rupee, and the result is stored on the charge, so a report never recomputes it.
19. **A money row is never edited or deleted.** Every Phase 3 tenant table carries
    `<table>_no_delete` and `<table>_no_truncate` triggers (`asms_forbid_delete`); amounts,
    parties and dates are frozen by `asms_forbid_columns_change` after insert; only the columns
    §3.2 names move, and only forward (`asms_forbid_change_once_set` where a column is set once).
    A void, a refund, a refund reversal, a carry-forward, a waiver, an adjustment, a shortfall
    write-off and a payroll correction are **new rows that reference the original** (rule 4).
    For people: **void means "this row is wrong"; to forgive a correct charge, adjust it or use
    a concession** — a voided generated charge never returns on its own (R179).
20. **Only verification credits the ledger** (rule 10). A `payment_claims` row and an open cash
    custody never appear in collections. Reports reconcile (R228): collections = non-voided
    receipts, refunds as their own line, net; outstanding = Σ `amount − allocated_amount −
    credited_amount` over open charges; Σ live allocations + Σ refunds (net of their reversals) +
    Σ carried forward (net of their reversals) + `unallocated_amount` = `amount` on every
    non-voided payment at all times; cash payments −
    voids before handover (a void in custody, never inside an open handover) = with collectors +
    handed over; counted − expected = surplus − shortfall, and a shortfall is recovered, written
    off or explained by a later void; a carried-forward payment and its reversal are their own
    report line and never collections, and so is a carry-forward undone. Each identity is a test over a scripted year.
21. **Separation of duties is a database invariant** (rule 13): nobody verifies their own claim,
    confirms their own handover, approves their own leave or expense, writes their own salary
    structure, advance or payslip adjustment, or acts on money for their **own child**
    (`actorIsGuardianOf`, R232). Each is a trigger *and* a service check with its own refusal
    reason; no capability or custom role switches it off. **One principal gate**,
    `requirePrincipal(session)` (R233), guards the verbs §3.1 lists; it reads the system role
    only — no grant satisfies it. **One exception, for a sole principal** (owner, 2026-10-06,
    R253): in a school with exactly one active principal, that principal may decide concessions
    and charge corrections for their own child, write their own salary structure and approve their
    own leave, each recorded `self_approved`; cash handling keeps no exception.
22. **Nothing is derived twice.** `charges.allocated_amount` and `credited_amount`,
    `payments.unallocated_amount` and `salary_advances.recovered_amount` are maintained by
    increment-under-lock triggers and bounded by plain CHECKs; outstanding is one function
    `amount − allocated − credited`; the leave balance, payslip net, salary pro-rating and
    student-count tier are each one pure function in `packages/shared`, table-tested.
23. **Money writes are online-only on the phone**, except a guardian's deposit claim (and its
    image) and an expense (and its receipt). `ONLINE_ONLY_ACTIONS` names the rest (§3.9); R162
    extends to them.
24. **No new capability keys, and finance keys are school-wide.** The eleven finance keys,
    `staff.leave.approve`, `staff.contract.manage`, `payroll.view`, `payroll.run` and
    `fee_head.manage` exist since Phase 1 (§7 of its plan); a grant gives school-wide scope and
    no finance route consults `Scope`. Teachers see no money: nothing in their role default
    reaches a fee, charge or payment route, and the plan does not recommend granting
    `payment.record` to a teacher.

### 1.3 Assumptions (each marked A*n*; correcting one changes the named slice)

- **A1** `charge-generate` runs **daily** (school clock) for the current period of every active
  academic year whose dates contain it; the 1st is simply its first run, and every later run is a
  catch-up for students admitted since (inserting only where no generated row exists at all,
  voided included). A manual run is idempotent; only `regenerateVoided` recreates a voided charge.
- **A2** The cut-off applies to monthly heads only (once and yearly heads per §1.1).
- **A3** A late fee attaches to the oldest overdue charge of the student for the period; never to
  a late fee, an adjustment, a campaign charge or a charge in a closed year.
- **A5** An advance is applied oldest-first to that child's later charges **of the same academic
  year** whenever one is inserted (generation, campaign, manual, late fee); until applied it is
  refundable per rule 20. It never crosses years by itself: the office carries it forward
  (R251), which needs an enrolment of that child in the target year.
- **A6** A concession applies to charges generated after its approval from `effective_from`;
  approving one with a past `effective_from` offers "apply to N open charges", which writes an
  adjustment per charge. A credit is never capped: beyond outstanding it de-allocates the charge's
  newest live allocations back into their payments as advances for the same student (never an
  admission-head allocation).
- **A7** Generation stops after `enrolments.ended_on`; a `suspended` student is charged (rule 20);
  dues survive withdrawal and transfer; the clearance check reads every enrolment of the student.
- **A8** Payment methods are `cash`, `bank_transfer`, `jazzcash`, `easypaisa` (and the internal
  `carried_forward`, never chosen at the counter); the office may record a non-cash payment from
  a slip the parent brings (verified on record, reference kept).
- **A9** Payroll's unpaid-absent days follow the leave matrix of R245; working days = calendar
  days − weekly offs − holidays with `applies_to_staff`.
- **A11** The platform bills **calendar months in advance** from the latest metrics row ≤ 7 days
  old on the 1st; platform payments are recorded by hand, full amount only (no gateway).
- **A12** The tier is the band containing the month's student count, unless the school is
  pinned. The monthly run sets `schools.sms_monthly_cap` to the tier's allowance **only while
  `sms_cap_overridden` is false**; a manual cap patch sets the flag; clearing it is an explicit
  action ("use the plan's allowance"); a change to a plan's allowance reaches schools at the next
  monthly run, not at once.
- **A13** A **collector** is any `payment.record` holder who records cash; every cash payment
  enters the recorder's custody; a `collection.handover.confirm` holder may open a handover on
  behalf of a collector who has left or is suspended, and then a third user confirms. A mistyped
  cash payment still in custody is voided by a `payment.void` holder who is not its recorder and
  leaves custody; inside an open handover it waits for the confirmation.
- **A15** The deposit image is the evidence of record; `verified_amount ≤ claimed_amount` (an
  upward correction is reject-and-resubmit); a lower amount needs a reason and the guardian is told.
- **A17** Expense categories are a code enum (the spec's list, `salary_advance_cash`,
  `cash_shortfall`, `other`); no category table.
- **A18** The platform's billing notices reach a school's principal through the school's own
  messaging, read from the non-tenant invoice table by `OwnInvoicesRepository`, whose only
  predicate is `school_id = schoolId` — the `OwnSchoolRepository` pattern, one import site.
- **A19** Jobs act as a **system actor** in the audit log: one row per run (counts, skipped
  classes) and one per auto-allocation, actor null, `metadata.job` set.

**`requirePrincipal(session)`** (`modules/access`, R233) reads `access.systemRoles.includes('principal')`
and answers `403 PERMISSION_DENIED { reason: 'principal_required' }`; no grant or custom role
satisfies it. `details.reason` values (as built): `principal_required | own_child | recorder | collector |
opener`.

**`actorIsGuardianOf(schoolId, actorUserId, studentId)`** (R232): true when the actor's user has
a guardian (merge-resolved) with a live link to the student. As a service check and a trigger it
refuses, with `409 SELF_ACTION_FORBIDDEN { reason: 'own_child' }`: concession create-approved and
approve, charge void, late-fee waive, adjustment, payment record (the payer guardian's user ≠
`recorded_by`), payment void, refund approval and claim verification; and, as a service check
only (the override is an audit row, no table to trigger on), the dues-clearance override, with
no sole-principal exception (2026-10-07).

**The sole-principal exception** (owner, 2026-10-06; R253). When the school has exactly one
active principal, that principal is not refused: concession create-approved and approve, charge
adjust, waive and void for their own child; their own salary structure; approval of their own
leave. Each writes `self_approved = true` (a column on `concessions`, `salary_structures` and
`leave_requests`; on adjustment, void and waive rows the existing `*_by` columns plus
`selfApproved` in the audit metadata) and appears on the dashboard tile "self-approved this
month" beside self-approved expenses. With two or more active principals every refusal stands.
**No exception for cash**: payment record, verify, void and refund for an own child, handover
confirmation and one's own salary advance stay refused for everyone. The service and the
triggers read the principal count as `expenses_not_self` reads the role, **under a lock on the
school's `school_settings` row**, so a second principal appointed concurrently cannot slip
between the count and the write. `CLAUDE.md` rule 13 gains this wording at the close.

  `asms_forbid_change_once_set`); `payment_allocations` — `reversed_at` once; `payment_claims` —
  the decision columns, `payment_id` (set on verify, cleared only under `asms.reversing_payment`),
  `reopened_at`, `image_*` (NULL → value once); `expenses` — `status`, the decision and void
  columns, `receipt_*` once; `cash_handovers` — the confirm and shortfall-resolution columns;
  `salary_advances` — `recovered_amount`, `status`, write-off columns; `leave_requests` — status,
  decision, cancel, `ended_early_on`, `cover_assignment_id`; `payslips` — nothing after the run is
  finalised (checked through the run), `status`/paid columns after; `receipts`, `receipt_lines`,
  `payment_reversals`, `payslip_lines`, `salary_advance_recoveries`, `charge_runs` (counts and
  status move) — append-only otherwise.
- **Counters by increment under lock, bounded by CHECKs** (never re-summed):
  `charges_allocated_amount_check (allocated_amount >= 0 AND allocated_amount + credited_amount
  <= amount)`, `charges_credited_amount_check (credited_amount >= 0)`,
  `payments_unallocated_amount_check (0 <= unallocated_amount <= amount)`,
  `salary_advances_recovered_check (0 <= recovered_amount <= amount)`. An AFTER INSERT
  statement trigger on `payment_allocations` (`REFERENCING NEW TABLE`) increments the charges and
  decrements the payments; the reversal trigger does the opposite and sets `reversed_at` where
  NULL; the adjustment trigger raises `credited_amount`; SQLSTATE 23514 maps to
  `CONCURRENT_UPDATE`, retried once by the service.
- **Lock order, everywhere** (R236): payment row → its charges `ORDER BY id FOR UPDATE` → the
  receipt counter, always last. The reversal trigger locks the charges in id order before
  updating; handover open locks the collector's payments by id; advance auto-allocation locks the
  payments first, then the charges; the generation job runs **one transaction per class**.
- **Transitions** (CHECK pairs each terminal status with its `*_at/*_by`): `charges` `open →
  settled | voided | waived`; `settled → open` only while `asms.reversing_payment` is set by the
  reversal trigger (the attendance `set_config(…, true)` precedent); `payments` `verified →
  voided`; `payment_claims` `pending → verified | rejected | withdrawn | expired`, `verified →
  pending` only under `asms.reversing_payment` (`reopened_at`); `expenses` `recorded →
  approved | rejected | voided`, `pending_approval → approved | rejected | voided`, `approved →
  voided`; `leave_requests` `pending → approved | rejected | cancelled`, `approved → cancelled`
  (before start) `| ended_early`; `payroll_runs` `draft → finalised`; `payslips` `pending →
  paid`; `platform_invoices` `issued → paid | void`; `charge_runs` `queued → running → done |
  failed`. **A crashed run never blocks a year**: the outbox sweep marks a run `failed` with
  `error_code = 'stale'` when it has been `queued` for more than 10 minutes or `running` for more
  than 15, and returns a campaign left `generating` to `draft`; runs are idempotent, so the next
  one is safe (R252).
- **Not-self, as triggers and service checks**: `payment_claims_not_self` and
  `leave_requests_not_self` (resolve `guardians.merged_into_id` to the survivor; read
  `asms.actor_user_id` on UPDATE), `payment_reversals_not_self` (void: requester ≠ recorder),
  `expenses_not_self`, `salary_structures_not_self`, `salary_advances_not_self`,
  `payslip_adjust_not_self`, and the own-child trigger of §3.1 (the concession, charge-correction,
  salary-structure and leave triggers admit the sole principal of §3.1 with `self_approved`; the
  payment, claim, reversal, handover and advance ones admit nobody); `cash_handovers` uses plain CHECKs
  `confirmed_by <> collector_user_id AND confirmed_by <> opened_by`, with `users @@unique([schoolId,
  id, staffId])` and a three-column FK binding the collector's user and staff.
- **No identity numbers** in references, descriptions, payee names, claim notes, reasons
  (`_no_id_check`); `@Reason()` (3–500) is the one reason validator.
- **Every money verb is audited** with amount, parties and reason (R230); jobs audit as the
  system actor (A19); the rule-24 refusal audit is deduplicated per user per day.

### 3.3 Pure functions (`packages/shared/src/money/`)

- `allocate(amount, openCharges, existingAdvances)` — consumes the student's advances first, then
  the new money, oldest `due_on` first, ties by charge id, over **exactly the named students**;
  returns allocations and the remainder. The service recomputes under lock and never trusts the
  preview.
- `outstanding(charge) = amount − allocated_amount − credited_amount`.
- `concessionAmount(gross, concession)` — `percentage` → `floor(gross × p / 100)`, `fixed` →
  `min(fixed, gross)`, **per charge of each named head**; only for an eligible head named by the
  concession.
- `lateFeeTarget(openCharges, settings, today)` — the oldest overdue monthly charge of the
  student for which no live late fee exists for its period.
- `dueOn(period, feeDueDay, createdOn, grace)` — `nominal = period-feeDueDay`; `createdOn >
  nominal ? createdOn + grace : nominal`. Only a charge created after its nominal date is born
  late; table-tested with `feeDueDay` 1, 5, 10, 28.
- `proRate(x, employedWorkingDays, workingDays)` — `floor(x × employed ÷ working)`; full when
  `workingDays = 0`.
- `unpaidDays(marks, approvedLeave, workingDays)` — the matrix below (R245 cites it).
- `absenceDeduction(basic, workingDays, unpaidDays)` — `floor(basic / workingDays) × unpaidDays`,
  0 when `workingDays = 0`.
- `payslipNet(...)` — absence first, then named deductions **in their listed order**, then
  advance recoveries; each stops before net goes negative; what a named deduction could not take
  is listed on the draft and **not carried**.
- `leaveBalance(entitlement, joinedOn, year, approvedDays)`, `tierFor(studentCount, tiers)`.

**The unpaid-day matrix** (per working day of the run's month; **attendance wins over leave
when the person was present**):

| Staff attendance mark | No approved leave | Approved **paid** leave | Approved **unpaid** leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| `present` or `late` | paid | paid | **paid** (not deducted) |
| `absent` | **unpaid** | paid | **unpaid** |
| `on_leave` | **unpaid**, listed on the draft so leave can be approved retroactively | paid | **unpaid** |
| unmarked | paid, counted in `unmarkedDays` | paid | **unpaid** |

A leave request spanning months counts only the working days inside the run's month.

### 3.4 Cash custody

Recording a cash payment writes it with `handover_id = NULL` (the recorder's custody). "Hand
over" (`POST /me/staff/cash-handovers`, by the collector, or `POST /cash-handovers` on behalf by a
`collection.handover.confirm` holder with `opened_by`, `on_behalf`) locks the collector's
non-voided custody payments by id and gathers them into one `open` handover with the **stored**
`expected_amount` (voided payments are already out). The confirmer — a third user when opened on
behalf — enters `counted_amount`; a shortfall or surplus is stored and a shortfall pushes the
principal.

**Voiding cash** (so a mistyped 5,000-for-500 never becomes a false shortfall): a cash payment
**in custody** (in no handover) may be voided by a `payment.void` holder who is **not its
recorder** (`payment_reversals_not_self`; the default holder is the principal); it leaves custody
and shows as `voidedBeforeHandover` on the daily cash report. The recorder can never void their
own payment, so the collector fraud path stays closed. A payment **inside an open handover**
cannot be voided (`409 PAYMENT_IN_CUSTODY`): confirm first; a void after confirmation shows as
`voidedAfterHandover`. A shortfall is resolved later by the principal, with a reason, as
`recovered` (dated), `written_off` (writing an expense of category `cash_shortfall` in the same
transaction) or `explained_by_void` (referencing the reversal row of a void made after
confirmation).

### 3.5 Receipts and print views

The receipt row is written in the payment's transaction, counter taken last (§3.2): numbers are
issued in commit order with no gaps, payments for one school and year serialise on the counter,
a voided receipt keeps its number and it is never reused. `receipt_<yearId>` is created in the
academic-year creation transaction (backfilled for existing years); `expense_no` at school
creation (backfilled). `SchoolCounterName` is widened to these names. The receipt snapshots its
lines (`receipt_lines`: student, head, period, year, amount; the advance line) and the WhatsApp/SMS
text names the school, number, amount, children and "balance for <year>"; never a link.

**`sendPrintView(res, html)`** (`common/`): `Content-Type: text/html`, `Content-Disposition:
inline`, `CSP default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'`, `nosniff`, `no-store`; an
auto-escaping template with no script; a test prints a `<script>` fee-head name as text (R237).
Receipts and payslips use it; the web opens it in the browser with the session cookie; **the app
renders `MyReceiptDto` and `PayslipDto` natively and shares them as text** — no WebView, no new
dependency. No PDF library.

## 4. Schema, complete for Phase 3 — the schema-freeze checklist

Every tenant table: `school_id NOT NULL` leading every index, `UNIQUE (school_id, id)`, the
`@ignore` School relation on both sides, the `school_id` immutability trigger, composite foreign
keys `(school_id, x_id)` each with a declared `@@index`, statuses as Prisma enums, CHECKs and

child or year than its enrolment; charge-linked rows reference `charges (school_id, id,

## 5. Slice plan

As built, the slice contracts in `contracts/slice-18.md` … `slice-27.md` supersede the DTO shapes

### Slice 19 — Charges: generation, admission fee, concessions, campaigns, late fees (≈ 7 days)

**Goal:** on the 1st every student has the month's charges once, concessions applied, proration

- **Generation (R179–R182, R240–R242).** The scheduled run is **daily**: on the 1st it charges
  the month; every later day it is a catch-up that inserts only for a student, head and period
  with **no generated row at all, voided rows included** (`NOT EXISTS` beside the `ON CONFLICT`),
  so a child admitted on the 5th is charged the next night and a voided charge never returns on
  its own. A manual run behaves the same unless `regenerateVoided` is true. For every active
  academic year whose dates contain the
  period: per class, one transaction; the eligible students are those with an enrolment in the
  class with `started_on ≤ end of period` and (`ended_on IS NULL OR ended_on ≥ start of period`),
  choosing per student the enrolment active on the 1st, else the first that started on or before
  the cut-off (the enrolment overlapping the period decides the cut-off); for every
  active monthly head with an active structure row effective ≤ period and `amount > 0`; `gross`
  from the structure, `concession` from the student's approved concession for the year whose
  heads include this one and `effective_from ≤ period`; `due_on = dueOn(period, feeDueDay,
  today, grace)` (the nominal date unless born late); a result of 0 is written `settled`.
  `INSERT … SELECT … ON CONFLICT (school_id, student_id, fee_head_id, period) WHERE kind =
  'generated' AND head_frequency = 'monthly' AND status <> 'voided' DO NOTHING`. Yearly heads: at
  the student's first generated period of the year, cut-off ignored, on `charges_yearly_key`.
  `per_term` and `ad_hoc` heads are never generated. Then advance auto-allocation (payments
  locked first, then charges), then `fee_charged` only for rows **this run** inserted, to
  families with a positive total. The run row (written for the 1st and for manual runs; for a
  catch-up only when it inserted something) records counts and the classes skipped for a missing
  structure. Budget **≤ 30 s at 3,000 students × 3 heads on the 1st; ≤ 5 s for a daily catch-up
  that finds nothing**. Wrong structure: void the charges, fix the structure, run with
  `regenerateVoided`; a manual re-run after a section rebalance inserts nothing.

## 7. Security-sensitive points and performance budgets

### 7.1 Security (the whole-phase review checks each)

- **Money tampering.** Amounts, parties and dates frozen by triggers; no delete on any table; the
  increment triggers bounded by plain CHECKs; status columns move forward only, `settled → open`
  and a claim's reopening only under `asms.reversing_payment`; receipts and payslips are
  snapshots; a replayed `Idempotency-Key` never records twice; every money verb audited with the
  amount; jobs audit as the system actor; the R57 table covers every new mutating route, payment
  accounts, fee-head `PATCH` and the late-fee settings included. A direct `UPDATE charges SET
  amount` through the Prisma client is refused by the trigger (test); the query guard still
  refuses an unscoped write.
- **Separation of duties** as triggers and service checks (§0.21): claims, handovers (collector
  and opener), leave, expenses, salary structures, advances, payslip adjustments, voids (not the
  recorder), and the **own-child** predicate on every money verb touching a student (R232). One
  principal gate for refunds, waivers, adjustments, generated-charge voids, shortfall resolution,
  advances and write-offs, the clearance override, direct concession approval and payment-account
  writes (R233); a grant never satisfies it. Finalising a run with one's own adjusted slip needs
  the principal.
- **Scope.** Finance keys are school-wide (rule 0.24); teachers reach no money route (R234). A
  guardian sees dues, receipts and claims only for live-linked children with `can_login`
  (`/me/children/:id/*`, R198); a receipt covering siblings shows a guardian their own linked
  children's lines plus the total; the claim image streams to the submitting guardian's user and
  `payment.verify` holders only; `/me/*` money DTOs are their own classes (`MyChargeDto`,
  `MyReceiptDto`, `MyClaimDto`) with no actor, collector, identity number or other family's
  data (R165 extended). `/me/staff/*` takes the staff id from the session only.
- **The platform never reads a school's ledger** (rule 1, R222): billing reads
  `platform_school_metrics`, `schools` and its own four tables; lint and an isolation test prove
  it; the per-school rollup writes one integer per day. The platform console shows invoices and
  counts, never a charge, payment, payer or family balance.
- **Sensitive images and documents.** Deposit slips carry bank account numbers: private bucket,
  sniffed and re-encoded, thumbnails from the re-encode pipeline, **streamed** (never a presigned
  URL) with `CSP sandbox`, `nosniff`, `no-store`, behind a capability or capacity check; never
  in a list, a push payload, a log or a message. Unique object keys per school.
- **Print views** through `sendPrintView` only: `text/html`, inline, `default-src 'none'`,
  auto-escaped, scriptless; a `<script>` fee-head name prints as text (R237). The app renders
  natively — no WebView.
- **Push bodies** for money types carry the title only (R238).
- **Identity numbers** cannot enter any new text column (`_no_id_check`); the R16 scan covers
  every new table and template.
- **Rate limits.** Claims 10/day per guardian and a daily upload cap; `payment-preview` 60/min;
  `finance-reports` 30/min; guardian money reads spend the `me-reads` budget.
- **Rule 24** in the guard, reading `password_is_default` per request, audited once per user per
  day (R225); extending it to money-out verbs is raised in §1.2.
- **No HTTP `DELETE`**; no row is deleted; a void is a row.

## 8. Numbered rules (each is a test)

**Fee setup**
- R176 Five fee heads are seeded in the school-creation transaction (backfilled for existing
  schools) with `created_by` null; heads are edited and archived, never deleted; at most one
  active tuition and one active fine head; a fine head is never concession-eligible and an
  admission head never refundable; an archived head keeps its charges and is skipped by generation.
- R177 A fee structure row is per (class, year, head, effective month) among active rows; a
  same-month replacement supersedes with a reason, a later month must be later than the latest
  active (`FEE_STRUCTURE_NOT_LATER`); past generated charges are untouched; a class without a
  structure for a head is skipped and named on the run; a row of 0 generates nothing.
- R178 Settings ranges hold; enabling late fees stamps `late_fee_enabled_at`; a settings change
  never rewrites existing charges, late fees or payslips.

**Charges and concessions**
- R179 Monthly generation is idempotent per (student, head, period) among non-voided rows
  (`charges_generated_key`, `ON CONFLICT` in the column-and-predicate form); the predicate picks
  one enrolment per student; a section change, a class change, a withdraw-and-readmit in one
  month and a re-run after a rebalance each yield one charge; the job runs one transaction per
  class inside the budget.
- R180 A student whose earliest enrolment start in the year is after `feeCutoffDay` is not charged
  that month's monthly heads; on or before it, they are; a mid-month class change charges the
  class active on the 1st.
- R181 A period is generated only inside the academic year's dates and only for enrolments
  overlapping it; the leaving month is charged in full; nothing after `ended_on`; a `suspended`
  student is charged; `withdrawn`, `transferred`, `alumni` keep their open charges.
- R182 An approved concession reduces the named eligible heads from its effective month:
  percentage rounded down, fixed capped at gross, never below zero; the amount is stored on the
  charge; the concession follows the student within the year and survives a section or class
  change.
- R183 A concession is requested by `charge.create`, decided by a principal holding
  `concession.grant`; a principal holding both keys creates it approved (`directApproval`); one
  live per student per year per head under a student-row lock; approving with a past
  `effective_from` may write one adjustment per open charge; ending affects future charges only;
  never for the actor's own child.
- R184 A campaign generates one charge per targeted enrolment through the shared resolver
  (student kinds only), deduped, concessions only when asked and eligible; cancelling a generated
  campaign voids nothing; generation is recorded as a `charge_runs` row.
- R185 With late fees enabled, each student with an overdue monthly charge (`due_on ≥
  late_fee_enabled_at`, enrolment not ended, past grace) gets exactly one late fee per period
  against the oldest overdue charge, under the fine head; only a principal waives it, with a
  reason and no allocations; a claim verified with `paid_on` inside the grace waives it in the
  same transaction when the verified payment settles the charge in full; voiding a charge voids its open late fee; disabling stops new ones.
- R186 A charge's amount, student, head and period never change; `charge.create` voids only
  manual and campaign charges, a principal voids generated and late-fee ones, always with a reason
  and no allocations; an adjustment is a new settled row that raises `credited_amount`, and a
  credit beyond outstanding de-allocates the newest live allocations into advances for the same
  student (never an admission-head allocation), with Σcredit = Δoutstanding + Δunallocated.

**Payments and receipts**
- R187 A payment is whole rupees > 0, one academic year, `Idempotency-Key` on record, with
  `verified_at = recorded_at` on office paths and a required reference for non-cash methods; a
  replay returns the same payment and receipt; `payerGuardianId` xor `payerName`; a walk-in names
  one student; with a guardian payer every student is live-linked.
- R188 Allocation spans exactly the named students, consumes their advances first, then oldest
  `due_on` first with id ties, excluding voided and waived charges, recomputed under lock in the
  §3.2 order; the increment trigger's CHECK refuses an over-allocation under a race and the
  service retries once.
- R189 A remainder is an advance bound to a named child (deferred constraint) and is applied
  oldest-first whenever a new open charge appears for that child, fines and late fees included;
  until applied it is refundable.
- R190 Every payment has one receipt numbered from the year's counter, taken last, gapless in
  commit order, immutable, printable through `sendPrintView`, sent to the fee-payer guardians
  with "balance for <year>"; a voided receipt keeps its number.
- R191 A void is a reversal row by `payment.void` whose requester is not the recorder, with a
  reason, refused while a refund exists or the cash is in custody; allocations are reversed under
  `asms.reversing_payment`, charges reopen, the receipt is marked void, a claim-backed payment
  reopens its claim.
- R192 A refund is a reversal of at most the unallocated amount, approved by a principal, with
  method and reason; a refund reversal is a further row; an admission-head allocation is never
  reversed into an advance; Σ live allocations + Σ refunds + unallocated = amount always.
- R193 Every cash payment enters its recorder's custody; a handover gathers them with the stored
  expected sum under lock; the confirmer records the counted amount; shortfall and surplus are
  stored, a shortfall pushes the principal, and resolution is `recovered`, `written_off` (an
  expense of category `cash_shortfall`) or `explained_by_void` (naming the reversal).
- R194 Nobody confirms their own handover or one they opened on behalf (CHECKs and service);
  an on-behalf open needs a collector who has left or is suspended.
- R195 The daily cash report's parts satisfy the §0.20 identities including voids before and
  after handover, cash refunds, cash expenses and cash salaries.

**Claims**
- R196 A claim exists only while the school has an active payment account; it is never a payment
  and appears in no collection; verifying records a payment with `received_on` = the verified
  paid date (the verifier may correct the guardian's date, ≤ the claim's date, with a reason; the
  claim keeps the guardian's), `verified_at = now`, allocation, receipt and any in-grace late-fee
  waiver read from the verified date, exactly as the counter; `verified_amount ≤ claimed_amount`;
  a lower amount or a corrected date needs a reason; the fee-payer guardians receive
  `receipt_issued` and the submitter sees the verified figures on the claim.
- R197 The verifier's user is never the submitting guardian's user (merge-resolved) nor the
  student's own guardian (service and trigger).
- R198 Dues, receipts and claim status are visible to every live `can_login` link of the child;
  the claim image only to the submitter's user and `payment.verify` holders; an ended link removes

- R199 `POST /me/uploads` accepts the guardian capacity under a daily cap; consumption is bound to
  the uploader; the app's claim and image lanes follow R157/R158 and delete the local slip once
  accepted; at most 10 claims per guardian per day, image-less ones included.
- R200 Verifiers are pushed once, when the image lands; an image-less claim cannot be verified
  and expires after 24 h.

**Reminders, defaulters, clearance, statements**
- R201 The due reminder goes once per fee-payer per period, naming every child and the total,
  only to families with open charges and no pending claim covering them, by SMS only if allowed
  and under the cap.

- R203 The defaulter list reads `allocated_amount` and `credited_amount` through the covering

- R205 Statements and the collections report show both dates; `basis` switches between them;

**Expenses**
- R206 At or below the threshold an expense is recorded; above it, approved only when the
  recorder is a principal (`selfApproved`, audited, on a tile), else pending; nobody else approves

- R208 Receipt images stream only to expense readers with the §7.1 headers; nothing

**Leave**
- R209 The balance is `entitlement (pro-rated by join month; full when joined_on is null) −

- R210 Nobody approves, rejects or ends their own leave (service and trigger); an on-behalf

- R211 Overlapping pending, approved or ended-early requests are refused by the exclusion

- R212 Approving a class teacher's leave may create the cover assignment in the same transaction

**Payroll**
- R213 A salary structure is a dated row with named components, superseded or closed by its
  successor, never written for the actor's own staff row, refused once a finalised payslip
  references it inside its month; a finalised payslip never changes when a structure does.
- R214 A run per calendar month, prepared for the previous month, takes active staff (suspended
  skipped and listed; left staff with a pending adjustment included) with a structure effective
  on the last employed day; basic and allowances are pro-rated over employed working days;
  `working_days = 0` means full pay and no deduction; `net ≥ 0` by CHECK.
- R215 An advance is recovered from `recover_from` in grant order at `min(instalment,
  outstanding)` per finalised payslip, stopping before net goes negative; grant and write-off are
  the principal's and never for their own row; a cash advance may be an expense in the same
  transaction.
- R216 A finalised run is immutable; a correction is a signed adjustment line in a later draft
  referencing the earlier payslip; an adjustment on one's own slip is refused; a finaliser whose
  own slip carries an adjustment needs the principal role.
- R217 A staff member reads only their own structure and payslips, with the staff id from the
  session; `payroll.view` reads all.
- R218 Marking a payslip paid records date, method and reference once; the payroll report's

**Platform billing**
- R219 Tiers are platform rows with non-overlapping active bands, a price and an SMS allowance;

- R220 The monthly invoice per non-trial, non-terminated school (suspended included) is

- R221 An invoice becomes overdue the day after `due_on` and suspension-eligible after the grace

- R222 The billing module reads `platform_school_metrics`, `schools` and its own tables only;

- R223 Assigning a plan sets `sms_monthly_cap` and clears the override flag; a later cap patch

- R224 Terminating a school stamps `terminated_at` (CHECK); the console shows the retention end

**Rule 24, mobile, reports, cross-cutting**
- R225 `role.manage` and `user.account.manage` routes are `403 DEFAULT_PASSWORD_BLOCKS_ACTION`
