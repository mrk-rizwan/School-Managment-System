Section 01

The whole picture

One system serves many schools. Each school is completely separate from every other.

You set a school up, and from that moment it has its own students, its own staff, its own money and its own records. No school can see another school's data — not by accident, and not on purpose. Each pays a monthly subscription to keep using the system.

Figure 01One platform, many separate schools
Adding a school is a setup step, not a new system. The tenth school costs the same to run as the second.

Section 02

How money works — two separate systems

There are two kinds of money here, and they never mix.

Schools pay you a monthly subscription to use the software. That is your income. Parents pay the school admission fees, monthly fees, exam fees and trip fees. That is the school's income — you only keep the records.

Keeping these apart matters more than it sounds. A school's fee income is its own business, must never appear in your accounts, and must never be visible to another school.

Figure 02Two money layers
If a school stops paying its subscription, its workspace becomes read-only. Its fee records, results and student history are never deleted.

The principal's phone

The principal does not carry a laptop around the school. Their phone shows what is waiting for them.

The mobile app is deliberately not a shrunken copy of the web system. A principal walking the corridor is approving things, not filling in forms. Anything that needs a keyboard — admissions, salary structures, report cards, settings — stays on the web.

Everything on this screen is an action, not a number to look at. A dashboard of statistics gets opened twice and then ignored; a list of things waiting gets opened every day.

  • Verify fee deposits — the screenshot, the amount and the student, with approve or reject in one tap
  • Approve staff leave — and see who will cover that class
  • Send a quick announcement — to the whole school or one class
  • Record an expense — photograph the receipt, type the amount, done
  • Today's attendance — students and staff, at a glance
  • Today's collections — what came in, and what is still with teachers
  • Contract expiry warnings — 30 days ahead, so nobody lapses unnoticed
  • Confirm a teacher's cash handover — when the office is closed
Recording an expense works without signal — the receipt photo and amount are saved on the phone and sent when the connection returns. Principals record expenses in storerooms and car parks.

Section 04

A day in the school

This is the rhythm that makes the system get used every day, not once a term.

Everything a teacher does in the morning reaches the parent the same day. That is the whole point — a parent should not learn in March that their child has been absent since January.

Figure 04From the teacher's phone to the parent, same day
Teachers mark attendance on their phone, for their own class only. Two kinds of mark, two rules: small class-test marks reach the parent straight away, while term and annual results stay hidden until the principal approves them (Section 08).

Section 05

Admitting a new student

The admission decides a student's whole financial profile, so it is recorded carefully. The fee decision made here quietly governs every invoice for years afterwards.

Figure 05From application to active student
If a parent already has a child in the school, the new student joins the same family record — one login, both children, no duplicate parent details.

Section 06

Fees, fines and charges

One system handles every kind of charge: monthly fees, exam fees, trip fees and fines.

The principal creates a charge, chooses who it applies to — the whole school, one class, one section, or named students — and the system creates one entry per student. If a teacher is asked to collect it, it appears in their app with their class list.

One rule that protects everyone

When a teacher collects cash in class, that money is in the teacher's hands, not the school's. Marking a student paid records the collection; the ledger is only credited when the teacher hands the cash to the office and the office confirms the amount. This protects the teacher as much as the school — the record shows exactly what was collected and what was handed over.

Figure 06Every way a fee gets paid
Nothing is ever deleted. A rejected deposit keeps the original screenshot and the reason — which is what settles a disagreement with a parent months later.

The school's own accounts

Beyond fees, the school records what it spends: salaries, building rent, electricity, purchases and daily expenses. Money in minus money out gives the profit report the owner actually wants.

Money in

Admission, monthly and exam fees, trips, fines — all recorded as they are collected.

Money out

Staff salaries, rent, utilities, repairs, purchases and daily expenses, each with who approved it.

Profit report

Per month, per term or per year. Built from the same records, so it always matches.

Section 07

Reaching every parent

This is the part most school software gets wrong, and it is the reason ASMS will work here.

Many parents do not have a smartphone. Many who do buy only a social bundle — WhatsApp, Facebook and YouTube — which cannot open a school app at all. Some have a keypad phone and nothing else.

So the app is never the only way a parent hears from the school. Every message goes out on the channel that actually reaches that parent.

Figure 07One message, three kinds of parent
Parents can also send fee deposit screenshots back over WhatsApp, so a parent with no app can still pay and be recorded properly.

On knowing who read it: the school can always see whether a message was delivered. Whether it was read is only knowable in the app, and on WhatsApp when the parent has read receipts switched on. SMS gives no read signal at all — so for a keypad-phone parent, delivery is the honest limit.

Section 08

Tests, results and certificates

Small tests through the term build into the term result. Nothing is entered twice.

  1. Teachers hold daily, weekly and monthly tests and enter marks from their phone as they go.
  2. The system combines them into the term result using the rule the school sets, so nobody adds up marks by hand.
  3. The principal reviews and approves the term result before it is published. A term or annual result is never visible to a parent until it is approved. Small class-test marks are a separate thing — those reach the parent on the day, as shown in Section 04.
  4. Report cards are generated and become available to parents and students in the app.
  5. Certificates are issued from the same records — school leaving, character, academic — each numbered and printable.

Every past year stays on record. A student who left three years ago can still have a leaving certificate printed with their real marks.

Section 09

What gets built

Two applications, not five. This is a deliberate decision that keeps the cost down and the quality up.

Web system

For the office and the principal. Admissions, fees, salaries, expenses, results, reports, configuration — everything that needs a keyboard and a big screen.

Mobile app

One app, different screens by role — the principal's approvals inbox (Section 03), the teacher's classes and diary, the parent's children. One install, one update, one thing to support.

WhatsApp & SMS

Not an app at all — the channel that reaches every parent regardless of phone or data package.

One mobile app instead of four means one thing to install, one thing to update, and one thing to support. A parent who becomes a teacher's assistant does not install a second app.

Section 10

What we need from you

Everything above is agreed in shape. The account and permission designs in sections 03 and 05 are our recommendation and await your confirmation. These few decisions are yours, and each one changes how the system behaves.

DecisionWhat we need to knowWhen
Discounted students A student on free or half fees — do they still pay exam fees, trip fees and fines? Or are those waived too? Before build
How results are combined Daily, weekly and monthly tests all exist. How much does each count towards the term result? The system cannot calculate a result until this is fixed. Before results are built
Moving up a year At year end, is every student promoted automatically, or does the result decide? What happens to a student who repeats, or who still owes fees? Before the first year-end
Attendance detail Is attendance marked once a day, or once per subject period? A daily record can never be split into periods later without rebuilding it, so this has to be right first time. Before build
Staff leave cover When a class teacher is on leave, who marks that class's attendance that day? Our proposal: on approving the leave, the Principal names a covering teacher who gets that class for those dates only. Confirm or change it. Before build
Part payments If a trip costs Rs 2,000 and a parent pays Rs 1,000, is that accepted and recorded as partly paid, or refused? Phase 3
WhatsApp number Does each school use its own WhatsApp number, or does everything come from one number for all schools? Phase 2
Profit reporting Should income count when a fee is due, or when it is actually received? These give different profit figures. Phase 3
