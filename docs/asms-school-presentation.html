An integrated administrative and academic management platform for schools — covering student records, attendance, examinations, fee collection, financial accounting, staff administration and parent communication.

System overview Slide 03

Architecture and access model

ASMS operates as a single platform serving multiple institutions. Each school's records are held in complete isolation from every other institution on the platform.

PlatformService providerInstitution setup, subscription administration, technical support
InstitutionYour schoolComplete operational independence. No visibility of, or by, any other institution
UsersSix rolesPrincipal, office staff, class teacher, subject teacher, parent, student

Governing principles

Principle 1

Data isolation

Institutional records are segregated at every level. No cross-institution access exists in any circumstance.

Principle 2

Records are permanent

No record is ever deleted. Corrections are recorded as additional entries referencing the original, preserving a complete audit trail.

Principle 3

Authority is delegated, not assumed

All administrative permissions derive from the Principal and may be withdrawn. Every permission change is logged.

Principle 4

Academic year scoping

All academic and financial records are bound to an academic year, permitting accurate historical reporting across sessions.

Sensitive records — identity documents, family CNIC data and financial information — are held under restricted access.

Attendance configuration Slide 07

Institutional variation in attendance practice

Practice differs between institutions and frequently between sections of the same institution. ASMS accommodates all recognised methods within a single configuration.

Institutional practiceSystem configurationResponsible officer
Single daily registerThe class teacher records attendance in the first period. This constitutes the attendance record for the day.Class teacher
Period-wise registerEach subject teacher records attendance for their own period. The daily position is derived automatically from the period records.Each subject teacher
Combined practiceDaily register for junior sections; period-wise recording for senior sections. Configured per class.Both, by class
Method of accommodation

Attendance is stored at period level and presented at whichever level the institution requires. An institution recording attendance once daily is never presented with period data. An institution recording each period obtains both views. The configuration may be revised subsequently without loss of historical records, which would not be possible were the system built to daily recording alone.

Consideration

Period-wise recording requires eight entries per teaching day rather than one. It provides materially greater value in secondary sections and is frequently disproportionate in primary sections. We recommend evaluating this per class rather than institution-wide.

This configuration must be determined before implementation. It is question 1 at slide 24.

Attendance procedure Slide 08

Late arrival

Attendance is recorded at 8:00 in the first period. A student arrives at 8:40. The following procedure applies.

Stage 1Initial markingStudent recorded absent in the first period, in the ordinary course
Stage 2Arrival recordedGate, office or teacher records the arrival and time
Stage 3Status revisedRecord amended to "late" — a distinct status, not "present"
Stage 4Guardian advisedAbsence advice suppressed; late advice issued instead if configured

Recommended configuration

  • Late is recorded as a distinct status. A manual register cannot distinguish habitual lateness from occasional absence; this produces a reportable record of both.
  • Arrival may be recorded by the gate or the office. The teacher is not required to reopen a closed register.
  • Absence advice is issued on a delay. Issuing at approximately 9:30 rather than 8:05 avoids alarming guardians of students who were merely delayed.
  • Amendments are permitted until the close of the teaching day and are recorded against the officer making them.
Institutional decision required

For the purpose of monthly attendance percentage, is a late arrival treated as present, as a half day, or as absent beyond a specified time? Practice differs and the determination affects every attendance report the institution produces. The same determination is required for early departure.

Fee procedure Slide 14

Payment by bank transfer or mobile wallet

Guardians increasingly remit by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash. The following procedure records such payments to an auditable standard.

Stage 1RemittanceGuardian transfers to the institution's account
Stage 2EvidenceConfirmation submitted to the institution's WhatsApp number
Stage 3PendingRecorded but not credited to the account
Stage 4VerificationOffice reconciles against the bank statement
Stage 5CreditedBalance updated, receipt issued
Rationale for the pending stage

A payment confirmation is a representation, not a receipt of funds. It may be a prior transaction, an incorrect amount, or a remittance to a different account. Until reconciled against the institution's statement, crediting the account would render the institution's records inaccurate. The procedure protects the institution, and equally protects the guardian, since rejected confirmations are recorded with a reason rather than disregarded.

Recommendation

We recommend that each fee demand carry a unique reference number for the guardian to include in the transfer narration. Reconciliation then requires seconds rather than investigation — a material consideration given that the remitting account name seldom corresponds to the student's name.

Direct in-application payment may be introduced subsequently. It carries a transaction charge and requires a merchant facility.

Fee procedure Slide 15

Collection in class where payment methods differ

A teacher is collecting Rs 2,000 from forty students for an excursion. One guardian intends to remit online instead. The following procedure applies to each student individually.

Teacher recordsSystem actionTeacher accountable
Collected in cashRecorded against the student. Funds are held by the teacher pending remittance to the office.Yes, until handed over
To be remitted onlineStudent remains outstanding. Guardian is issued the account particulars and reference number.No
Not paidRemains outstanding. Recovery correspondence issued in the ordinary course.No
Principle

The teacher's accountability extends only to funds physically received. Upon the guardian remitting online and the office verifying the transfer, that student is removed from the teacher's outstanding list automatically. At no point is a teacher required to account for funds that did not pass through their hands.

Remittance to the office

The office counts the funds received and confirms the amount against the teacher's recorded collections. Where a shortfall arises, students remain credited with the amounts they paid. The difference is recorded as a matter between the institution and the member of staff, and is reported to the Principal. A student's fee record is not adjusted to reconcile an administrative discrepancy.

Collection, remittance and confirmation are each recorded with the officer and time.

Communication strategy Slide 19

Ensuring correspondence reaches every guardian

This consideration determines whether an institutional communication system succeeds. An application-only system reaches solely those guardians holding a smartphone with an active data facility. A substantial proportion do not.

Guardian circumstanceConstraintChannel employed
Smartphone with dataNoneApplication notification, supported by WhatsApp
Social media package onlyData package permits WhatsApp and social platforms but cannot access any applicationWhatsApp
Feature telephoneNo application, no WhatsAppSMS, with particulars set out in the message body
Method of operation

The institution composes one message. The system determines the appropriate channel for each guardian according to their recorded circumstances. The application supplements the primary channels; it is never the sole means by which an institution reaches a guardian. Urgent notices — closure, emergency — are issued by WhatsApp and SMS without exception.

Limitation, stated plainly

The institution may always establish whether a message was delivered. Whether it was read is ascertainable within the application, and on WhatsApp where the recipient permits read confirmation. SMS provides no read confirmation whatsoever. No system can provide this; we state it rather than imply otherwise.

Guardians may submit fee confirmations through the same channel, requiring no application.

Governance Slide 21

Data security and institutional ownership

Ownership

Records belong to the institution

Institutional data remains the property of the school throughout and following the subscription period, and may be exported.

Isolation

No cross-institution access

Records are segregated at every level. No institution can access, or become aware of, another institution's data.

Sensitive data

Restricted categories

Identity documents, guardian CNIC records, student photographs and financial records are held under restricted access.

Audit

Attributable actions

Every entry, amendment and approval records the officer and the time. Corrections supplement rather than replace.

Continuity

Backup and recovery

Records are backed up daily to separate infrastructure, with restoration tested rather than assumed.

Support access

Governed and logged

Where technical support requires access to institutional data, such access is governed by agreement and recorded.

On termination

Should the institution discontinue the subscription, records are retained for an agreed period during which they may be exported in full. Records are not withheld as a condition of continued subscription.

Terms governing support access and retention are set out in the service agreement.

The system is configured to institutional policy. The following determinations are required; the first six are necessary before configuration commences.

Summary Slide 25

Distinguishing characteristics

Design

Developed for this market

B-Form, CNIC, Urdu, rupee accounting, feature telephones, WhatsApp and in-class cash collection are addressed as primary requirements, not as adaptations.

Reach

Not application-dependent

WhatsApp and SMS reach every guardian. Systems that assume smartphone access exclude a substantial proportion of the parent body.

Configuration

Adapted to the institution

Financial integrity

Corrections supplement the record. The institution can establish what occurred and when, at any subsequent date.

Commercial

Monthly subscription
